How do B2B, Wholesale & Institutions differ from E-Commerce Marketplaces?

The Core Difference: On e-commerce marketplaces like Shopify or WooCommerce, every business operates the same way—customers browse a product catalog, add items to a cart, and pay immediately at checkout. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What counts as a "sale we help close"?

You only pay the small % commission when a customer actively chats with your Yield Force AI assistant or clicks a Yield Force checkout/campaign link and completes their purchase within 7 days. Regular store orders that had no interaction with Yield Force are never charged.

2. What happens if a customer returns or cancels an order?

You never pay commission on cancelled or refunded orders. Commission is calculated strictly on Net Product Sales (excluding shipping and taxes) with a 14-day return reconciliation window.

3. Are there any surprise AI or token fees?

No. Your Monthly Base includes all AI usage up to your plan's monthly conversation limit (500 on Capture, 2,500 on Compound, Unlimited on Command). You do not need to buy a separate OpenAI or Google AI subscription.

4. Who pays for my Meta/TikTok ad spend and WhatsApp number?

You connect your own Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and WhatsApp Business accounts directly. That means your ad budget and Meta's standard WhatsApp message rates are billed directly by them at cost—with zero markup from Yield Force.
